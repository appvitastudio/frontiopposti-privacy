# Privacy Policy - Fronti Opposti: Civil War

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Fronti Opposti: Civil War ("the game") is a tactical action game published by **AppVita Studio**.
This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your game data stays on your device
Your campaign progress, choices, settings, rank, experience points and statistics are stored **only on your device**,
in the game's private storage. We do not collect them, we do not transmit them and we have no servers that receive them.
There is no account and no registration. The game works fully offline.

The faction you choose (Fronte Nazionale or Unione Popolare) is a fictional gameplay preference only. It is stored on your
device and is never collected, shared or used as information about your real opinions.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**. Ads are **never shown during a battle**,
only between battles. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the advertising identifier of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

No game data (progress, choices, settings) is shared with the advertising network.

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK)
If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you a consent form from Google's
User Messaging Platform **before any ad is loaded**. You choose whether to allow personalised advertising. If consent is
required and not given, no ads are requested at all. You can change your choice at any time from Options > Privacy options.

## 4. Permissions
- **Internet**: used only to load ads and the consent form. The game itself works fully offline.
- **Advertising ID**: used only by Google AdMob as described above.

## 5. Data we do not collect
We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any content of your play.
We use no analytics SDK of our own.

## 6. Children
The game contains fictional military violence and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## 7. Your rights
Since your game data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it: uninstalling the game, or clearing its
storage from Android Settings > Apps, deletes all of it. For data processed by Google for advertising, see the Google links
above; you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID from Android Settings.

## 8. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
